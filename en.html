Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Planix+ is a personal planning and organization application that allows users to manage tasks, notes, lists, orders, personal financial information, and other content related to daily organization.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use Planix+.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Planix+ may process the following types of information:

Account Information

To create and maintain your account, we may process information associated with your account, such as:

  • Email address.
  • Internal user identifier.
  • Information necessary to authenticate and maintain your session.

User authentication is performed through Firebase services.

User-Created Information

Planix+ may store information that you voluntarily enter into the application, including:

  • Tasks and routines.
  • Notes.
  • Lists and checklists.
  • Shopping lists.
  • Orders and products.
  • Information about income and expenses.
  • Dates, times, categories, and other data related to your tasks.
  • Information that you choose to share with other Planix+ users.

This data is used exclusively to provide the application's functionality.

Contacts

Some features may allow you to select contacts from your device to facilitate actions such as sharing information or using contact details.

Planix+ only accesses contacts when you expressly authorize such access through the device permissions.

Access to contacts is used to provide the functionality requested by the user and does not mean that your entire contact list is permanently stored on our servers.

Speech Recognition

Planix+ may offer voice-input features using speech recognition.

When you use this feature, spoken content may be processed by speech-recognition services available on your device or operating system. The operation and processing of this data may also be subject to the privacy policies of the applicable provider.

Notifications

Planix+ may use notification permissions to send reminders related to your tasks, routines, and other application features.

You can manage or disable notifications through your device settings.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Planix+ is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Save and synchronize your tasks and other content.
  • Allow access to your data through the application.
  • Allow you to share certain content with other users when you request it.
  • Display reminders and notifications.
  • Provide widgets and other application features.
  • Improve the stability and operation of Planix+.
  • Maintain account and data security.

Planix+ does not use users' personal content to sell information to third parties.

3. Storage and Third-Party Services

Planix+ uses third-party technology services to provide certain functionality.

These may include Google Firebase services, used for features such as authentication and data storage.

Data stored through these services is also subject to the applicable security measures and policies of the respective providers.

You can consult the privacy policies of the services used by Planix+ on their respective official websites.

4. Sharing Information with Other Users

Some Planix+ features allow you to share information with other people.

When you choose to share a task, note, checklist, order, or other content, that information may be accessible to the users with whom you decided to share it.

Planix+ does not automatically share your private content with other users. Shared content depends on actions and settings made by the user.

5. Financial Information

Planix+ may allow you to manually record information related to income and expenses.

This information is used to provide organization and financial-control features within the application.

Planix+ is not intended to be a financial institution, bank, financial adviser, or banking service provider.

You should not enter bank information, full card numbers, banking passwords, or other sensitive financial credentials into fields that are not specifically intended for them.

6. Planix+ Widget

Planix+ may provide a widget to display application information directly on your device's screen.

The widget may display information such as pending tasks and routines according to the user's settings and permissions.

The widget is not an independent service and is part of the Planix+ application.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by Planix+ against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or improper disclosure.

However, no data storage or Internet transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Planix+ functionality or while the user's account remains active, unless a legal obligation requires certain data to be retained for an additional period.

Where applicable, users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and the data associated with it.

When a deletion request is made, the data that should be deleted will be removed, except for information that must be retained for legal, security, or compliance reasons.

To request deletion of your account or data, you may use the contact method provided at the end of this policy.

10. Device Permissions

Depending on the features used, Planix+ may request certain device permissions, including:

  • Notifications.
  • Contacts.
  • Access to speech-recognition-related functions.
  • Other permissions necessary for specific features.

Permissions are requested only when necessary to provide the corresponding feature.

You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

11. Children's Privacy

Planix+ is not specifically designed for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without the appropriate authorization from their parents or legal guardians.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Planix+ inappropriately, you may request its deletion using the contact method provided below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to Planix+, its features, the services used, or applicable legal requirements.

When significant changes are made, the date shown at the beginning of this policy will be updated.

We recommend reviewing this page periodically for any changes.
